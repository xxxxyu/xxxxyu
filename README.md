# Hi, I'm Xiangyu Li (李翔宇)

I'm a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate at the [Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University](https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), advised by [Prof. Yunxin Liu](https://yunxinliu.github.io) and working closely with [Prof. Ting Cao](https://tingcao952.github.io).

I work on **on-device AI** and **embodied AI**, with a current focus on efficient deployment of embodied foundation models (VLAs and WAMs) and self-evolving physical intelligence.
